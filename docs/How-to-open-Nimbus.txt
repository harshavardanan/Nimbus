HOW TO OPEN NIMBUS
==================

1. Drag "Nimbus" into your Applications folder.

2. Double-click Nimbus.
   macOS will say it can't be opened, or can't check it for malware.
   That's expected: Nimbus is a free app that isn't registered with Apple yet.
   Click "Done" (don't click "Move to Trash").

3. Open  Apple menu  >  System Settings  >  Privacy & Security.
   Scroll down to the Security section. You'll see a message that Nimbus
   was blocked. Click "Open Anyway" and enter your Mac password.

4. Double-click Nimbus again and click "Open".

You only need to do this once.


WHERE IS IT?
Nimbus has no Dock icon. Look for its small icon at the top right of your
menu bar. Move your mouse over the notch to open it.
Click the gear inside it to change how it looks.

It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or newer.


STUCK?
Open the Terminal app, paste this line, press Return, then open Nimbus again:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Nimbus.app
